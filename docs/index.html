Show the steps.Not just the result.

A step recorder for explaining a task the way you would to your grandma. Every click becomes a screenshot, a note, and a guide someone else can follow.

What it records

Open Chrome, come to this page, and follow the directions. A voice walks through each one.

A step per clickEach click is a new step, in the order you did it.
A screenshotThe screen is captured so the next person can see what you saw.
A click circleA circle marks where you clicked, in the app and on the export.
What was clickedOn Windows, the recorder names the control when it can.
A commentAdd a sentence between steps when the click is not obvious.
A guide to shareHand someone the result instead of walking them through it again.

Take it with you

The same recording can leave as a page, a document, notes, or a video.

HTMLAn interactive step-by-step page.
PDFA printable copy of the steps.
MarkdownText with the screenshots embedded.
VideoAn optional MP4 of the actions.
WindowsWindows 10 and later.
macOSmacOS 10.12 and later. Screen recording permission is required.

The report

This is the document Grandma saves when you click Save. Every step has the time, what was clicked, and the screenshot.
