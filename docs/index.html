Step recorder

Show the steps.Not just the result.

Explain a task the way you would to your grandma. Every click becomes a screenshot, a note, and a guide someone else can follow.

Download for Windows Watch the walkthrough

What it records

Windows and macOS. You click through the task. Grandma writes the guide.

A step per clickEach click is a new step, in the order you did it.
A screenshotThe screen is captured so the next person can see what you saw.
A click circleA circle marks where you clicked, in the app and on the export.
What was clickedOn Windows, the recorder names the control when it can.
A commentAdd a sentence between steps when the click is not obvious.
A guide to shareHand someone the result instead of walking them through it again.
HTMLPDFMarkdownVideoWindows 10+macOS 10.12+

The report

This is the document Grandma saves. Every step has the time, what was clicked, and the screenshot.
